The place

A neighborhood coffee house with a lot going on.

Mismatched furniture, local art on every wall, and a shelf of books nobody remembers donating. Coffee, food and smoothies, made in-house.

Blue Danube has been a neighborhood fixture in San Francisco for years — the kind of spot where regulars have a usual table and the art on the walls changes more often than the menu.
